# Maintainers and Review Roles

## Project leader

- **Manish Tripathy** — `manish.tripathy@owasp.org`

The project leader owns the OWASP relationship, repository access, release approval, and escalation. The authoritative leader record is the OWASP project page.

## How roles work

Roles are assigned through a public governance decision with a declared scope, conflict statement, and review date, as described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Until a role is separately assigned, project leaders hold it.

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Requirement and testability maintainer | Reviews requirement wording, acceptance tests, and assessment consistency |
| Safety and rights reviewer | Reviews privacy, human-rights, safeguarding, and misuse implications |
| Schema and validation maintainer | Owns schemas, fixtures, validation, and release checks |
| Release manager | Owns manifests, versioning, change logs, and release evidence |
| Accessibility reviewer | Reviews documentation and asset accessibility |
| Translation and regional coordinators | Own localisation and regional review |

## Open invitations

The project actively welcomes contributors for each role above, and particularly values reviewers from different organisations, regions, and legal contexts. Candidates should have relevant experience in OSINT practice, intelligence analysis, privacy or human rights, safeguarding, standards development, data modelling, or accessibility.

To volunteer, open an issue describing your background, the role you are interested in, and any conflicts of interest. Sustained contribution is the usual route to a named role.
