# OOSIS Initiative Charter

## Identity

- **Initiative name:**
- **Proposer and proposed lead/co-leads:**
- **Status:** Proposed / Incubating / Active / Paused / Retired
- **Initial review date:**

## 1. Problem and global relevance

Define the problem, who experiences it, why it is globally relevant, and where scope differs by region, language, sector, or legal environment.

## 2. Mission outcomes

State the decision, protective outcome, intelligence capability, or community benefit that should improve. Include measurable success signals.

## 3. Deliverables and non-deliverables

List exact artefacts: standard sections, testing scenarios, mappings, schemas, synthetic datasets, tools, guides, translations, workshops, or reference implementations. State what the initiative will not attempt.

## 4. Evidence and validation plan

List primary sources, datasets, independent reviewers, exercises, benchmark approach, implementation feedback, and known evidence gaps.

## 5. Team and community

Name lead/co-leads, maintainers, reviewers, required expertise, recruitment needs, public meeting/contact route, and conflicts of interest.

## 6. Delivery plan

Provide quarterly milestones, a zero-cost path, dependencies, publishing plan, and review/retirement criteria.

## 7. Globalisation and accessibility

Explain language/translation priorities, regional review, low-resource access, accessibility needs, and examples/datasets that avoid a single-country bias.

## 8. Safety and disclosure

Explain how the initiative handles sensitive data, live cases, vulnerable people, dual-use content, vulnerability disclosure, and contributor safety.

## 9. Approval request

Identify the programme assets affected, requested decision, and expected first public update.
