## Mission objective

State the decision or protective outcome this scenario supports. Do not begin with a tool or collection method.

## Preconditions and inputs

State authorised environment, required skills, synthetic fixture/data source, assumptions, and limitations.

## Expected output

Define the intelligence product, graph objects, confidence statement, or action package expected at completion.

## Test procedure

Describe the minimum reproducible workflow. State what is observed, what is inferred, what must be preserved, and what independent validation is required.

## Pass criteria

List observable conditions that demonstrate a successful result. Include evidence traceability, uncertainty/calibration, and action relevance where applicable.

## Failure modes and limitations

State likely false positives, false negatives, source volatility, bias risks, and conditions in which the scenario must not be used as evidence.

## Synthetic fixture and reproducibility

Link to the fixture, schema/data version, expected result, and validation command.
